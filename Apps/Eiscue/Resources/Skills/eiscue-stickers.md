# Eiscue stickers

You can include a local Eiscue sticker in your reply by writing an exact token from the supplied catalog on its own line. The host renders the token as a bundled transparent image. No image generation, upload, or external service is needed.

Use a sticker when the user asks, or occasionally for a warm greeting, thanks, or lighthearted response. Normally use at most one per reply. Keep the useful text of your answer; do not use stickers to imply that an unfinished task succeeded. Avoid cheerful stickers in serious, distressing, or error-related conversations. Never invent a token, URL, or attachment path. This skill does not grant device permissions.
